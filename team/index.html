The Cartographers

The people behind the maps. ReGovNet is a small group of researchers from Public Administration, led by Shuwen Zhang — studying how regions and local governments govern together, and building the methodological tools to study it well.

What we study

Our substantive work sits where public organizations meet across boundaries — the regional and intergovernmental spaces where single jurisdictions no longer suffice.

Regional Governance

How metropolitan regions, city-regions, and cross-border areas coordinate policy when no single authority is in charge — institutions, leadership, and performance.

Substantive focus

Governance Networks

Public agencies, nonprofits, and firms woven into networks of collective action — how structure shapes collaboration, trust, and outcomes.

Substantive focus

Interlocal Management

The everyday machinery of cooperation between local governments — service agreements, shared delivery, contracting, and the manager's craft of making collaboration work.

Substantive focus

How we study it

The group works as its own methodology shop: every argument we make is expected to survive a serious design. These are our home tools.

Causal Inference

From quasi-experimental designs — difference-in-differences, regression discontinuity, synthetic control — to matching and modern causal machine learning: identifying what actually works, not just what correlates.

Methodological strength

Social Network Analysis

Exponential random graph models, network autocorrelation, and visualization — treating the web of relationships itself as the unit of analysis.

Methodological strength
