No gurus, no miracle supplements: if it is on these shelves, someone tested it.

A note of care. The evidence shelf is educational, not medical advice — it complements professional care; it never replaces it. If anxiety persists for more than two weeks, impairs your work or sleep, or comes with panic attacks or thoughts of self-harm, please contact a licensed mental-health professional or your institution's counseling service. Asking for help early is what the evidence would prescribe.

Suggest a Remedy — or a Field Note
